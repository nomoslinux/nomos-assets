# Asset provenance

This file is the publication gate for binary assets distributed with NomOS.

**Do not make the repository public while any shipped asset is marked
UNVERIFIED.**

For each asset, record enough information to show why NomOS may redistribute
it: the original source (or "NomOS original"), the author/rights holder, and
the applicable licence or permission. Keep source links when one exists.

A repository-level software licence is not evidence that a third-party image
may be redistributed under that licence.

For Gemini-generated images, the recorded basis is Google's current Terms of
Service: Google does not claim ownership over original content generated by
services that support generation. This is provenance/redistribution evidence,
not a representation that every AI-generated image necessarily attracts
copyright protection in every jurisdiction.

written in the row above. If that basis cannot be established, replace or
remove the asset before publication.
